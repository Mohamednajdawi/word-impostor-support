Privacy Policy

Word Impostor · Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the Word Impostor mobile app ("the App", "we") handles and why. The App is a pass-and-play party game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following is saved locally on your device so the game works the way you left it. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Player names you type in for a game
  • Game settings (language, timer, sound, vibration)
  • Custom word categories you create
  • Local game statistics (games played, wins)

You can delete this data at any time from Settings in the App, or by deleting the App.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

iPhone and iPad: the current iOS version shows no ads, does not ask to track you and collects no data. If ads are added to iOS in a future version, this policy will be updated first.

Android: the App is free and shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob between games. To serve, limit, and measure ads, Google may collect:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (on iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking)Ad serving, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement
Approximate location derived from your IP addressShowing regionally relevant ads
Ad interactions (views, taps)Ad measurement and performance
Diagnostic data (crashes, performance of the ad SDK)Keeping the ad SDK working

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive data that identifies you personally.

3. Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): The current iOS version never tracks you. If a future version adds ads, iOS will first ask whether the App may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states): Where required by law, a Google consent form asks for your choices before ads are personalised. You can change your choice anytime via Settings → Ad Privacy Choices in the App.
  • Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

4. Children

The App is a general-audience party game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the App are restricted to family-appropriate content ratings.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share any data ourselves. The only third party that receives data from the App is Google AdMob, as described above.

6. Security and retention

Game data stays in the App's private storage on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Mohamednajdawi/word-impostor-support/issues.
