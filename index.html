Word Impostor

The pass-and-play party game where one of you doesn't know the secret word.

How to play

  1. Pick a mode, the number of players (3–20) and impostors, and a word category.
  2. Pass the phone around. Each player privately sees the secret word, unless they're an impostor.
  3. Take turns giving one-word clues. Impostors must bluff without knowing the word.
  4. Vote for who you think the impostor is. A tie means nobody is voted out and the impostors escape.

FAQ

Do I need internet?

No. The game works fully offline. Ads only load when you're online.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts or sign-in.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you're in a region that requires consent, open the App's Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

How do I delete my data?

All game data is stored only on your device. Clear statistics from the App's Settings, or delete the App to remove everything.
